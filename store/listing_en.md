## Full description · max 4000 chars
```
🍒 Drop a fruit. Touch a matching one. They merge into the next bigger fruit!
🍓 Start from a tiny cherry — and 11 stages later, make the giant watermelon.
🍉 When two watermelons meet, the board pops in a satisfying finale.

▶ Simple one-hand controls
Drag your finger across the top of the play area to choose where to drop the
fruit, then release. Anyone can pick it up in under a minute — and then can't
put it down.

▶ Real physics simulation
Every fruit rolls, bumps, and stacks with believable weight. Cluster matching
fruits together to set up huge combo cascades.

▶ 11-stage evolution chain
Cherry → Strawberry → Grape → Tangerine → Persimmon → Apple → Pear → Peach →
Pineapple → Melon → Watermelon. Each stage features its own candy-style
illustration and a juicy "pop" effect.

▶ Combo system
Chain merges back-to-back and score multipliers climb. Set up the perfect
trigger and watch a tower collapse into one massive combo.

▶ Watch the LIMIT line
If a fruit sits above the LIMIT line for more than ~1.5 seconds, the round
ends. Every drop matters — clean play wins.

▶ Casual, offline, ad-free
The game works completely offline. No ads. No in-app purchases.
Perfect for a quick round during lunch, commute, or right before bed.

▶ You might love this if you...
• enjoy short, snappy puzzles with great tactile feedback
• love satisfying combo / merge / cascade mechanics
• want a clean, ad-free experience
• know the "just one more round" trap and embrace it

Start your watermelon journey now with Fruit Pop!

---
※ This app does not collect or transmit any personal information. All data is
   stored locally on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.
```
