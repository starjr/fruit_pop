# Fruit Pop

A pastel-themed fruit-merging puzzle game built with Flutter.
Drop fruits, merge identical ones, and chain combos all the way to a watermelon.

- **Bundle ID**: `io.starjr.fruitpop`
- **Source code**: [github.com/starjr/fruit_pop](https://github.com/starjr/fruit_pop)
- **Privacy policy**: [Privacy Policy](./privacy/)
- **Contact**: [starjr@gmail.com](mailto:starjr@gmail.com)

## Highlights

- 11-tier fruit progression (cherry → watermelon)
- Custom 2D physics — gravity, collision, resting state, tipping
- Game over rules: LIMIT line crossing, or two-watermelon merge
- Local-only persistence (no server, no analytics, no ads)
- Daily challenges, skin shop, in-app privacy policy viewer
- Korean localization

## Privacy at a glance

The app does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All
in-game data (scores, coins, settings) is stored locally on your device only.

For full details see the [Privacy Policy](./privacy/).
